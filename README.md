# MLT Study Cards

Interactive study cards for Medical Laboratory Technology students,
based on the course books by Dr. Tarek Faour (revised edition, 2027).

**Open the cards:** https://drtarekfaour.github.io/study-cards/

## Books
- Hematology (301 cards)
- Clinical Hematology & Hemostasis (221 cards)
- Serology & Blood Banking (345 cards)

## How to use
Pick a book, then a chapter. Think of the answer first, then check yourself.
Card types: flip cards, sorting, reaction grids, antibody panels,
ordering steps and calculators. Cards marked "Again" can be reviewed later.
Progress is saved on your own device.
